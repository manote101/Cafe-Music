# Cafe Music Prompts

พรอมป์ต์ภาษาอังกฤษสำหรับสร้างเพลงด้วย AI เช่น Suno หรือ Udio ทุกเพลงเป็นดนตรีบรรเลง ไม่มีเสียงร้อง เหมาะสำหรับพักผ่อน อ่านหนังสือ หรือทำงาน

## 1. ร้านญี่ปุ่นริมป่าไผ่ในวันฝนตก 🎋🌧️

อารมณ์: สงบ ละมุน มีพื้นที่ให้ความคิดค่อย ๆ คลี่คลาย

```text
Instrumental Japanese ambient cafe music, 65 BPM. Soft felt piano, delicate nylon-string guitar, sparse koto plucks, warm upright bass, and barely audible brushed percussion. Gentle rain on the cafe roof, distant bamboo leaves rustling in the breeze. Intimate wooden cafe beside a misty bamboo forest, a warm coffee cup by the window. Minimal melodies, spacious phrasing, soft dynamics, warm organic recording. Peaceful and quietly reflective, suitable for journaling, reading, and focused work. Let the rain blend naturally beneath the instruments. No vocals, no dramatic crescendos, no sharp percussion or sudden changes. Seamless loop with a gentle opening and ending.
```

## 2. ร้านกลางเมืองที่มองเห็นสวนสาธารณะ 🏙️🌳

อารมณ์: ผ่อนคลายอย่างมีชีวิตชีวา ช่วยให้ทำงานได้เพลิน

```text
Instrumental contemporary cafe jazz with a gentle neo-soul groove, 82 BPM. Warm Rhodes electric piano, clean hollow-body jazz guitar, rounded electric bass, subtle brushed drums, and occasional soft vibraphone accents. A stylish, comfortable city cafe overlooking a large green park through floor-to-ceiling windows. Relaxed urban sophistication, warm afternoon sunlight, fresh coffee, and an easy sense of flow. Smooth extended jazz chords, understated melodies, steady light rhythm, uncluttered arrangement. Suitable for working on a laptop and taking slow coffee breaks. No vocals, no busy solos, no heavy bass, no loud snare hits. Keep the energy consistent and the transitions smooth. Seamless loop.
```

## 3. ร้านริมหน้าผา มองเห็นภูเขาหิมะในยุโรป 🏔️🔥

อารมณ์: อบอุ่น ลึก และโปร่งกว้างเหมือนวิวขุนเขา

```text
Instrumental alpine chamber jazz and acoustic ambient music, 60 BPM. Warm felt piano, gently fingerpicked acoustic guitar, soft sustained cello, and restrained upright bass. Almost no percussion. A cozy timber-and-stone cafe perched above a mountain valley, panoramic views of snow-covered European peaks, a quiet fireplace, and steaming coffee. Intimate close-miked instruments with a subtle spacious reverb suggesting the mountains beyond the window. Slow unfolding harmonies, tender melodies, calm warmth, and a sense of quiet wonder. Add very faint fireplace crackle beneath the music. No vocals, no epic orchestration, no dramatic build, no strong rhythmic accents. Gentle, continuous, loop-friendly ending.
```

## 4. ร้านโต๊ะไม้และชั้นหนังสือในฤดูใบไม้เปลี่ยนสี 🍂📚

อารมณ์: อบอุ่น ชวนคิดถึง เหมาะกับการอ่านหนังสือยาว ๆ

```text
Instrumental autumn acoustic cafe music with soft folk-jazz influences, 72 BPM. Fingerstyle acoustic guitar, mellow upright piano, warm double bass, delicate brushed drums, and occasional muted clarinet phrases. A quiet book-filled cafe with wooden tables, amber reading lamps, and windows overlooking golden, orange, and red autumn leaves. Cozy, thoughtful, gently nostalgic, with a comforting sense of an unhurried afternoon. Simple memorable motifs with subtle variations, soft tape warmth, natural room acoustics, and plenty of breathing space. Suitable for reading and sustained concentration. No vocals, no prominent page-turning sound effects, no busy improvisation, no sudden changes. Smooth seamless loop.
```

## 5. ร้านริมน้ำในเมืองเก่าจีน 🏮🌿

อารมณ์: อ่อนโยน เรียบสง่า ไหลเรื่อยเหมือนสายน้ำ

```text
Instrumental Chinese-inspired acoustic cafe music with subtle jazz harmony, 68 BPM. Delicate guzheng phrases, softly played xiao bamboo flute, warm piano, gentle upright bass, and very sparse brushed percussion. A small elegant cafe in a preserved Chinese river town, a wooden table directly beside a wide open window with a clear view of the slow-moving river, an old stone bridge, and historic tiled rooftops. Paper lanterns and a steaming coffee cup create a warm, intimate atmosphere. Graceful pentatonic melodies blended naturally with warm contemporary chords. Intimate, understated, peaceful, and flowing, like watching a small boat drift past while sipping coffee. Very faint water ambience beneath the instruments. No vocals, no ceremonial drums, no gongs, no theatrical flourishes, no piercing flute notes. Soft dynamics throughout and a seamless loop.
```
